i'm mohammad jafari — mo to most people. i'm a software engineer in
toronto, ontario, canada, and i build products from zero to
production: infrastructure, ai pipelines, and the full stack in
between. this site is my own, written by hand, and it's the canonical
place to find out what i'm working on.

## what i do

i'm the founding engineer at
[verlo](https://verlo.finance),
where we're building an ai crm for wealth management. i own the stack:
aws infrastructure, llm and retrieval pipelines, and integrations with
schwab, fidelity, and the meeting platforms advisors actually live in.
we're soc 2 certified, which mostly means i've learned more about
audit evidence than i ever planned to.

before verlo i built
[retellio](https://retellio.com)
end to end — the whole platform, from the aws account up through the
rag pipelines and the salesforce, hubspot, and gong integrations, at
99.9% uptime. before that i was a senior software developer at
[nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com),
where i helped ship their first generative ai product, an entity
research copilot now used by more than 2,000 financial institutions. i
owned the retrieval and evaluation modules. earlier still, i spent two
years at
[verafin](https://verafin.com)
building fraud detection agents and machine learning pipelines in
java, starting as an intern and getting promoted twice.

## background

i studied computer science at
[memorial university of newfoundland](https://www.mun.ca)
in st. john's, graduating in 2023, and worked as a developer the whole
way through — including two years rebuilding the student portal for
15,000+ users. i work in python, typescript, go, and swift, on aws,
docker, kubernetes, and terraform. these days most of my code gets
written by directing ai rather than typing, and i
[write about what that's actually like](https://mohammad.page/i-havent-coded-in-3-months.html).
