# privacy - mohammad jafari

> What mohammad.page collects, why, and how to opt out. Google Analytics, a theme preference in localStorage, and nothing else.

Source: https://mohammad.page/privacy.html

---

Last updated August 22, 2026

this is a personal site run by me, mohammad jafari, in toronto,
ontario, canada. there are no accounts, no forms, no newsletter, and
nothing to buy. i don't sell or share data about you, because i barely
have any. here's the whole picture.

## what gets collected

**analytics.** the site loads google analytics 4
(measurement id G-40C7CVW886) to count page views. google receives
your ip address, user agent, referrer, and which pages you opened, and
sets its own cookies to tell one visit from another. i only ever look
at the aggregate: which posts people read, roughly where readers come
from. i can't identify you from it and i don't try to.

**your theme choice.** if you switch between the light,
dark, and watercolor themes, that choice is saved in your browser's
localStorage under the key `theme`. it never leaves your
device and it isn't sent anywhere. clearing your site data removes it.

**server logs.** the site is hosted on github pages and
served through their cdn. github logs requests, including ip
addresses, under
[their privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
i don't have access to those logs.

## third parties

the [spotify page](https://mohammad.page/spotify.html) embeds players from
open.spotify.com, so spotify sees those requests and applies its own
privacy policy. outbound links to linkedin, github, x, and elsewhere
are ordinary links — those sites take over once you click. fonts and
images are served from this domain, so no font cdn sees you. there are
no ads, no trackers beyond analytics, no session recording, and no
third-party pixels.

## if you'd rather not be counted

any tracker blocker, or a browser that blocks third-party scripts,
will stop analytics from loading; the site works exactly the same
without it. google also publishes an
[opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
nothing on this site is gated behind consent because nothing here
needs it to function.

## agents and crawlers

automated readers are welcome. [robots.txt](https://mohammad.page/robots.txt)
allows everything except two deprecated site versions kept in the repo
for reference, and [llms.txt](https://mohammad.page/llms.txt) describes what's
here and when it's worth citing. requests from agents are logged the
same way any other request is, and no more.

## your rights, and reaching me

if you're in canada, the eu, the uk, or anywhere with similar rules,
you can ask what data relates to you, ask for it to be deleted, or
object to the analytics processing. in practice the only place your
data sits is inside google analytics and github's logs, and the
fastest route is the opt-out above. either way, email
[me@mohammad.page](mailto:me@mohammad.page) and i'll do what
i can.

if this policy changes i'll update the date at the top. the source
history for this page is public on
[github](https://github.com/mossein/mossein.github.io),
so you can see exactly what changed and when.
