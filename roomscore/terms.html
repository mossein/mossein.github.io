1. What RoomScore Does

You capture photos of a room, or scan it with your device's LiDAR sensor. Those photos, along with room measurements or geometry derived from the scan, are sent to our backend service, which forwards them to a third-party AI provider (Google's Gemini API) for analysis. The App then displays a score, suggestions, and — where available — optional AI-generated "makeover" images based on that analysis.
