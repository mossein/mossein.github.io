about

i'm mohammad jafari — mo to most people. i'm a software engineer in toronto, ontario, canada, and i build products from zero to production: infrastructure, ai pipelines, and the full stack in between. this site is my own, written by hand, and it's the canonical place to find out what i'm working on.

what i do

i'm the founding engineer at verlo, where we're building an ai crm for wealth management. i own the stack: aws infrastructure, llm and retrieval pipelines, and integrations with schwab, fidelity, and the meeting platforms advisors actually live in. we're soc 2 certified, which mostly means i've learned more about audit evidence than i ever planned to.

before verlo i built retellio end to end — the whole platform, from the aws account up through the rag pipelines and the salesforce, hubspot, and gong integrations, at 99.9% uptime. before that i was a senior software developer at nasdaq, where i helped ship their first generative ai product, an entity research copilot now used by more than 2,000 financial institutions. i owned the retrieval and evaluation modules. earlier still, i spent two years at verafin building fraud detection agents and machine learning pipelines in java, starting as an intern and getting promoted twice.

background

i studied computer science at memorial university of newfoundland in st. john's, graduating in 2023, and worked as a developer the whole way through — including two years rebuilding the student portal for 15,000+ users. i work in python, typescript, go, and swift, on aws, docker, kubernetes, and terraform. these days most of my code gets written by directing ai rather than typing, and i write about what that's actually like.

what i write about

my blog is where i think out loud about shipping llms in production, deleting code, ai hype cycles, and occasionally heidegger. it's not a content strategy; it's whatever i was chewing on that month. if you want the short version of what i'm doing right now, there's a now page.
