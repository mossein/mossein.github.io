Product leaders don't have hours to replay customer calls. At

## Why insight podcasts

Teams get overwhelmed by volume. The signal is in there; objections,
unmet jobs, surprise use cases; but it's buried across Zoom and Gong
recordings, and scattered notes. Our goal is simple: surface the
moments that matter so decisions happen faster.

## How it fits your stack

- Plug in your tools: integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, and
Zoom.

- Distribute where decisions happen: share to Slack or attach
summaries and clips to CRM records.

- Trace every insight: each conclusion links back to the source moment
in the call.

## What's under the hood

We segment calls into moments, rank them for relevance, and generate
structured summaries grounded in the original audio. The pipeline
emphasizes determinism and evidence over free-form prose, so leaders
can trust what they're hearing.

## Anatomy of an episode

- Theme intro that sets context in under 20 seconds.

- Top moments with timestamps and one-line takeaways.

- Contrarian or outlier segment to avoid echo chambers.

- Risks and unknowns called out explicitly with source links.

- Actions and owners so follow-ups don't get lost.

## Ranking what makes a moment matter

- Cross-account frequency and recency to separate noise from signal.

- Sentiment shifts and stated intent changes within a call.

- Role weighting so buyer pain outranks curiosity from non-decision
makers.

- Proximity to revenue, including opportunity stage and renewal risk.

- Competitive mentions, blockers, and workaround hacks that hint at
unmet jobs.

## A quick story

A fast-growing team at FactoryFix faced an avalanche of calls. With
insight podcasts, they started catching recurring pains earlier and
routing clips straight into their product and sales workflows.
Leadership listened on the go, product managers tagged follow-ups with
linked evidence, and sales pulled key objections into enablement;
accelerating decisions without more meetings.

## Build notes

We ingest from Zoom and Gong, transcribe and diarize, detect moments,
embed for retrieval, then generate structured summaries with
source-grounding before optional voice rendering. We emphasize
determinism controls, PII redaction, and SOC 2 Type 2–aligned handling
throughout the pipeline.
